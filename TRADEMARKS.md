# AltSpacy name and logo

The code is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE): you can use, study, change and share it. The name **AltSpacy**
and the AltSpacy logo are different. They identify this project and its official releases, and the license does not
grant rights to use them (an additional term under GPL-3.0 section 7(e)).

**You may, without asking:**

- Share unmodified official releases under the AltSpacy name.
- Say that your project is "based on AltSpacy" or "a fork of AltSpacy", and link here.
- Use the name to talk about the project, in reviews, articles, tutorials and so on.

**Please don't, without permission:**

- Name a modified version, fork or derived product "AltSpacy", or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the AltSpacy logo as the icon or branding of a modified version. Forks should replace the logo
  (`src/AltSpacy/Logo.cs` draws it) and the name shown in the app.
- Suggest that a modified version, or any other product or service, is official or endorsed by this project.

When you distribute a modified version, the GPL also requires that you keep the copyright and license notices,
including the ones shown in the About window, and mark your changes.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue in this repository.
